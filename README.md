# Socio-Demographic Profile of CTTE-Adopted Villages

### Evidence from Unnat Bharat Abhiyan Survey Data

A comprehensive rural data analytics project focused on analyzing the socio-economic, demographic, and infrastructural conditions of villages adopted under the **Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA)** initiative. This project transforms real-world household survey data into meaningful insights using **Python, Excel, and Power BI** for data-driven rural development planning. 

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes survey data collected from selected villages in the **Ponneri block of Thiruvallur district, Tamil Nadu**, including:

* Orakkadu
* Panchetty
* Sothuperumbedu
* Jaganathapuram
* Aathur

The project focuses on understanding rural demographics, infrastructure availability, sanitation conditions, agricultural dependency, and awareness of government welfare schemes through statistical analysis and visualization. 

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze demographic and socio-economic conditions of rural households
* Study literacy levels and occupational patterns
* Examine infrastructure facilities such as water supply, electricity, and sanitation
* Evaluate awareness and utilization of government schemes
* Perform statistical analysis using Python and Excel
* Develop interactive dashboards using Power BI
* Generate insights for sustainable rural development

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Agriculture is the primary livelihood source for most households
* Moderate literacy levels with limited higher education access
* Uneven sanitation and waste management facilities across villages
* Electricity and water facilities are available in most households
* Low awareness and utilization of government welfare schemes
* Infrastructure development varies across villages
